# Base-builder guide requires personal knowledge of a mobile-base jump

`spacemolt-docs/guides/base-builder.md`, heading "Plan the journey and the escort":

> The commander needs **ManageBases** and personal knowledge of the destination.

`spacemolt-docs/changelog.json` release `0.713.4`:

> Faction mobile bases can now jump to any adjacent public system, even if the commander has never visited it.

> Jumps to frontier systems still need the commander or the faction to have charted the destination. The `unknown_system` error now says this.

Change that sentence. An adjacent public system does not require a visit. A frontier destination must be charted by the commander or the faction. Do not keep "personal knowledge" as the rule for every destination.
